Who we are

West Coast builders who get the job done.

Aitking Builders Ltd is a Hokitika-based general contracting company delivering quality building work across the West Coast. Whether you're planning a new build, a full renovation or a structural repair, we bring the same level of craft and commitment to every project.

We know the West Coast — the conditions, the communities, and what it takes to build something that stands up to it all. If you want it done right the first time, you want Aitking Builders.
